WHAT AND WHY

  While the crate is cold, each cron tick was costing three Discogs
  calls, not one:

    1. authenticated attempt        -> 429
    2. retry after backoff          -> 429
    3. anonymous fallback           -> 429

  The retry and the fallback were written for the request path, where
  a visitor is waiting and it is the only shot. On a per-minute cron
  the next shot is sixty seconds away, so a cold tick was burning
  three slots of exactly the shared headroom it is hunting for.

  warm() now always calls the producer with first=false: one plain
  attempt per tick. 1440 calls/day instead of ~4300. The request path
  is untouched — a cold visitor still gets retry + anonymous fallback.

  To be clear about proportion: this was never the cause. Readings
  were already 78-88 before any cron existed. Our share is a few
  percent of a bucket that sits around 80 on its own. This is about
  not being part of the problem, not about fixing it.

TESTS ADDED

  - and stores nothing
  - a cold visitor request still tries all three
